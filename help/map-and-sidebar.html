World map

The map shows the satellite sub-satellite point (where it is over the Earth), its ground track, and a footprint (the area on Earth where the satellite is at or above your minimum elevation from Settings → Tracking). Your station is marked on the map.

  • Click a satellite label or icon to select it.
  • Tab to the map, then use arrow keys to change the selected satellite.
  • Coloured footprints help you see which satellites are near your horizon.
  • A small arrow inside each footprint shows the direction the satellite is moving along its ground track.

Sidebar — Live

Shows data for the selected satellite, updated every second (UTC clock at the bottom).

ItemMeaning
Standby / ResumeBrowsing mode: parks rotator, pauses CAT. After Resume, click a pass or satellite to focus again. While in Standby, menu Rotator opens manual az/el control.

Sky plot

A polar view as seen from your station: centre is overhead, the outer ring is the horizon. Click a satellite to select it, same as on the map. Expand or collapse the section with the Sky plot header — OscarWatch remembers which you prefer.

Upcoming passes

A list of predicted passes for your enabled satellites. Times use your local display; the footer shows UTC. Click a row to select that satellite. Passes above your minimum elevation (Settings → Tracking) are included. On smaller windows the sidebar scrolls so you can reach the pass list; the list itself scrolls when there are many passes.

Badges on the right of a pass row:

BadgeMeaning
Orange countdownPass starts within about 15 minutes (time until AOS)
Green PassingPass is in progress (between AOS and LOS)
Red RECAudio is being recorded for this satellite (Settings → Recording)

Rows may also be highlighted in amber (soon) or green (in progress) behind the text.

Status bar

Shows how old the TLE data is and how many satellites are enabled. If TLEs are very stale, refresh them before relying on pass times.
